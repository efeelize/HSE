## 1.1 Moral Expectations（教材 p21–31）

### 1）总结
> care everyone:workers,contractor,visitor,public
> care everything:mental,physical,mistake,report,investigation
> ⭐ value:human life > productivity/profit
> leaders do something

### 2）考试重点（Claude 补充）

> ⭐ 管理安全的三大理由：**道德 moral（1.1）、经济 financial（1.2）、法律 legal（1.3）**。题目常问其中一个，别把三个混在一起写。
> 道德理由的要点：雇主对员工和所有受影响的人负有注意义务；社会不接受用安全换利润；要特别照顾弱势人群（孕妇、年轻工人、残障人士）；事故给员工和家属带来痛苦。
> 常见错误：道德题里写成本和罚款，那是经济和法律理由。

> 📖 ⭐⭐⭐ 教材补充（p28–29）：常考题型 "Comment on the organisation's health and safety morals"：根据场景推断组织在不在乎人。句型：The organisation appears to care about …, as shown by …；Management do not appear to value …, as …
> 📖 教材补充（p26–27）：看一个组织在不在乎安全，**看行动不看口号**。好的表现：做风险评估并落实、安全作业制度真正执行、投入时间和钱、培训和入职培训、做好应急准备、检查和维护设备、主动和被动监控、关心所有受影响的人、领导露面、事故少并吸取教训。差的表现：流程过时、追责文化、不培训不维护、管理层不露面、事故多且不吸取教训、士气低落、为产量牺牲安全。
> 📖 教材补充（p24–25）：事故对人的后果。短期：痛苦、死亡、收入减少、额外开支、残疾；长期：心理伤害、失去身份认同、生活质量下降、影响家庭；更广的影响：家人、同事、雇主、整个社会（ILO 估计约占全球 GDP 的 3.94%）。
> 📖 教材补充（p30–31）：ILO《工作中的基本原则和权利宣言》有 5 项基本原则，2022 年新增第 5 项"安全健康的工作环境"，所有成员国都要尊重，即使没有批准相关公约。
> 📖 English (p29): The organisation appears to care about worker safety, as shown by its investment in training and supervision.

> English:
> - Employers have a moral duty to protect workers, because no one should be harmed by the work they do.
> - Society expects employers not to put profit before the health and safety of workers.

## 1.2 The Financial Costs of Incidents（教材 p32–37）

### 1）总结

> ⭐ insured costs:1)工伤/职业病->ELI(雇主责任险);2)医疗、康复；3）建筑、厂房、设备损坏；4）第三方财产损失、第三人损害->PLI(公众责任险)；5）民事诉讼费用，compensation
> ⭐ uninsured costs:大部分损失

> direct costs
> indirect costs:1)disruption,业务中断；2）inefficient；3）商业机会；4）reputation

### 2）考试重点（Claude 补充）

> ⭐⭐ 高频大题：事故成本（10 分、20 分）。答题框架：先分 **insured / uninsured**，再按 **direct / indirect** 展开。
> 未投保成本通常**远大于**投保成本（"冰山"）：罚款和起诉费用（**不能投保**）、停工和生产损失、病假工资、调查花的时间、低于免赔额的维修、临时工和培训、加班、丢失订单和客户、声誉受损、士气低落、保费上涨。
> 投保成本：员工赔偿（ELI，但可能要付**免赔额 excess**）、公众索赔（PLI）、建筑和设备损坏（财产险）。
> ⚠️ 教材更正（p35）：**刑事辩护律师费通常没有保险**，罚款不能投保；索赔还会让**保费上涨**。处理事故花掉的**管理层时间**也是成本。
> 直接成本 vs 间接成本（p37）：直接成本是马上看得见的账单（医疗、病假工资、维修、清理、免赔额、罚款和律师费、临时工）；间接成本是之后才感受到的（停工、效率下降、管理层时间、招人和培训、声誉受损、丢失客户、士气低落）。
> 常见错误：把罚款算成投保成本；2 分题写 5 点（几分写几点就够）。

> English:
> - Uninsured costs could include lost production while the incident is investigated, sick pay for injured workers, and fines, which cannot be insured.
> - Compensation paid to injured employees is usually covered by employers' liability insurance.

### Q.List possible costs of workplace accidents that are insured. (2)

- 1.工伤或职业病赔偿金 Compensation for employee injuries or ill-health: Payouts awarded to injured workers or their families (covered by Employers' Liability Insurance).
- 2.医疗与康复费用 Medical and rehabilitation expenses: Costs for hospital treatment, surgeries, or physical therapy covered under insurance policies.
- 3.建筑、厂房或设备损坏 Damage to buildings, plant, or equipment: Repair or replacement costs for physical assets damaged during an incident (covered by property/equipment insurance).
- 4.第三方财产损失或人身伤害 Third-party property damage or personal injury: Claims made by members of the public or outside contractors who are hurt or suffer losses due to the company's operations (covered by Public Liability Insurance).
- 5.法律辩护费用 Legal defense costs: Fees associated with legal representation during insurance claims or civil lawsuits.

### Q:In the event of a failure of the crane, what financial costs to the organisation might be insured? (2)

- 1.起重机自身及公司财产损坏 Damage to the crane itself and company property: Repair or replacement costs for the damaged crane, lifting gear, or surrounding site structures (covered by plant/equipment or property insurance).
- 2.第三方财产损失 Third-party property damage: Costs to repair or replace property belonging to others that was damaged when the crane failed (e.g., hitting a neighboring building, vehicles, or public infrastructure), covered by Public Liability Insurance.
- 3.第三方人身伤害或死亡索赔 Third-party personal injury or fatal claims: Compensation payouts, medical bills, and legal defense costs if members of the public or outside contractors are injured or killed by the crane collapse (covered by Public Liability Insurance).
- 4.员工工伤或死亡索赔 Employee injury or fatal claims: Compensation and medical expenses if company workers are injured or killed, covered by Employers' Liability Insurance.

### case study:A Forklift Fatality Witnessed By a Dozen People

> A manufacturing organisation suffered a fatality when an employee was crushed by a forklift.The site was shut down for the rest of the day. The enforcement agency and police investigated.A dozen witnesses were off sick for a week due to the trauma. Counselling was arranged at the company’s expense.Morale was low for months and productivity was reduced. The organisation missed several production deadlines and lost one key account.Temporary labour was brought in to help and these people needed additional training and supervision.The company was eventually fined the equivalent of £300k and the insurance paid a substantial sum to the victim’s family. Legal fees amounted to £55k. The prosecution found the Managing Director had ignored several warnings from the H&S Team and subsequently left the company with his reputation damaged.

- Insured Costs: Payouts to the victim’s family (covered by Employers' Liability Insurance) and legal defense fees (£55k).
- ⚠️ 教材更正（p35）：刑事辩护律师费（£55k）**通常没有保险**，应归入 uninsured costs。
- Uninsured (Hidden) Costs: Fines (£300k - note: while legal fines are technically financial penalties, NEBOSH classifies them and prosecution costs as uninsured), lost production deadlines, loss of a key account, hiring and training temporary labor, sick pay for witnesses suffering from trauma, counselling expenses, and management turnover with reputational damage.
- Management Failure: The Managing Director ignoring warnings highlights a severe lack of leadership commitment and a breakdown in the safety management system.

## 1.3 What Enforcement Agencies Do and What Happens If You Don’t Comply（教材 p38–44）

### 1）总结

> ⭐ 看清楚问的是power 还是actions
> ⭐ actions（从轻到重）:1）verbal advice or warning；2)发出整改令（Improvement Notices）（限期纠正安全缺陷）;3)发出禁止令 / 停工令（Prohibition Notices）（立即勒令关闭危险作业）;4)提起诉讼 / 法庭审判（Prosecution / Court Proceedings）
> ⭐ powers: 来现场检查的：进入，inspect，investigate，取样测量、保持现场、询问，录口供、调阅文件

### 2）考试重点（Claude 补充）

> ⭐⭐ 区分题：**powers（调查时能做的事）≠ actions（查出问题后的处理）**，写错类别不得分。
> powers：进入工作场所（可不事先通知）、检查和调查、测量／取样／拍照、要求保持现场不动、询问人员和录口供、查阅并复印文件。
> actions 怎么选：违法但不紧急 → improvement notice（限期整改）；有严重且迫在眉睫的危险 → prohibition notice（立即停工）；严重违法或不执行通知 → prosecution（起诉）。
> 题目写 "based on the scenario only" 时，只写场景里实际出现的内容。

> 📖 教材补充（p40）：执法机构的 5 项职能：向政府提供立法建议、编写合规指南、主动检查工作场所、事后调查严重事故、采取执法行动（必要时起诉）。
> 📖 教材补充（p41–42）：powers 还包括：要求清除危险设备或物品、要求协助和使用设施、要求体检、发出法律通知。
> 📖 教材补充（p40）：C155 第 9 条要求国家建立检查制度、规定足够的处罚；第 10 条要求给雇主和工人提供指导。
> 📖 教材补充（p44）：国际标准（如 ISO 45001）是自愿采用的，但能统一做法，企业也可以通过供应链推动使用。

> English:
> - The inspector used the power to enter the site without notice and to examine the vehicle.
> - As there was no imminent danger, an improvement notice was appropriate rather than a prohibition notice.

## 1.4 The ILO and Their Conventions/Recommendations（教材 p45–50）

### 1）总结

> 雇主责任：
> ⭐ C155(成员国有约束力)场所（workplace）,设备（machinery,equipment）,物质（chemical,physical,biological）;PPE
> ⭐ R164(更具体的，无强制力):安全的场所，指示（train）、培训(induction)、监督（supervise）、组织、安排；记录（records accidents & incidents）, H&S management system,工时、休息、跟上新知识、免费提供PPE、消除过度疲劳

> 员工义务
> C155:配合雇主
> R164:1)自己/他人安全的合理的注意义务；2）遵守+发现情况及时报告；3)正确使用设备；4）报告：hazards,dangers,injuries,illness

> 员工权利
> ⭐ C155:1）撤离危险（不受处罚，危险未消除前不被要求返回）；2）知情（enough info）;3)培训（proper training）；4）协商（ask,discussion,unions help）
> R164:1)safety committees;2)进入现场（access to all parts of work）;3)带薪（reasonable paid time）

### 2）考试重点（Claude 补充）

> ⭐ C155 是公约，对**批准的国家**有约束力；R164 是建议书，没有约束力，只补充细节。公约约束的是国家，不直接约束企业。
> 题目点名问哪份文件，就只写那份。R164 独有的内容：**监督、工时和休息、消除疲劳、跟上新知识**。
> ⭐ 工人最重要的权利：遇到严重且紧迫的危险可以撤离，**不受处罚**。
> 三方机制（tripartism）：政府、雇主、工人三方协商。

> 📖 教材补充（p46）：C155 第 17 条：两家以上单位在同一场所同时作业，必须协作（连 1.8）；第 18 条：雇主要做好应急和急救安排。
> 📖 教材补充（p47）：雇主还应开展风险评估，来满足这些要求。
> 📖 教材补充（p49）：工人的培训权包括：刚入职时、引进新机器或新流程时都要培训；工人不为安全措施付费。

> English:
> - Under C155, workers who remove themselves from serious and imminent danger must be protected from undue consequences.
> - R164 states that employers should provide adequate supervision and make sure working hours do not cause excessive fatigue.

### case study

> In South African mines, there are health and safety representatives (HSRs) chosen by workers and a joint committee[联合委员会] made up of both employer and worker members. These groups help the employer talk to workers and sometimes make decisions together about health and safety at work.HSRs are usually part of trade unions and report to these unions. They are chosen as the mine health and safety agreement says. This agreement is signed by both the trade unions at the mine and the employer, as required by the Mine Health and Safety Act.HSRs play a key role, especially when it comes to the right of workers to refuse to work in dangerous conditions. Workers are more likely to leave a risky workplace if an HSR tells them to, rather than deciding to leave on their own.HSRs who have technical knowledge and are committed to getting reliable information can talk more effectively with employers about health and safety. They can bring their expertise to higher-level discussions about these issues.

## 1.5 Where You Can Find Information on National Standards（教材 p50–53）

### 1）总结

> ⭐ shall/must（必须）->practicable（只要可行，不看其他的因素）->reasonable(正常人的合理注意)->reasonably practicable(cost,time,trouble,and efforts.).
> ⭐ 去哪里找国家标准的信息：acts / decrees / regulations / orders（有法律效力）→ codes of practice 工作守则 → guidance notes 指引 → leaflets 宣传册 → statistics 统计数据；以及执法机构在检查（inspections）和跟进检查（follow-up visits）时提供的信息。

### 2）考试重点（Claude 补充）

> ⭐ 义务的三个等级要会区分：absolute（不论成本）→ practicable（技术上可行就要做）→ reasonably practicable（把风险和成本权衡，除非代价与风险严重不成比例）。
> ⭐ SFAIRP 答题时要写出权衡：措施的成本 vs 风险的大小。
> 信息来源题（Identify）直接列：法律法规、工作守则、指引、宣传册、统计数据、执法机构的检查和回访。

> 📖 教材补充（p51–53）：国家标准信息的 5 类来源：①法律（acts、decrees、regulations、orders）；②官方指南（标准、经批准的工作守则）；③执法机构（网站、专项行动、检查和回访、事故调查、执法行动）；④**法院判例**；⑤**其他来源**：专业团体、行业协会、保险公司。

> English:
> - Fitting guard rails is reasonably practicable, as the cost is low compared with the risk of serious injury.
> - Information on national standards can be found in legislation, codes of practice, guidance from the enforcement agency and accident statistics.

## 1.6 Roles of Directors, Managers, and Supervisors（教材 p53–57）

### 1)总结

| role | responsibility |
| --- | ---- |
| 最高层 top managers = directors 董事 + senior managers 高层管理者 | 1)制定方针、明确责任：create defined roles and responsibilities for directors, managers, supervisors, workers, H&S team；2)总负责：ultimately responsible for H&S；3)制定计划：create H&S policy, set and achieve objectives；4)提供资源：provide sufficient resources；5)确保、审查：H&S system, risk assessments, H&S reports & acts；6)明确责任：hold them accountable and ensure they have access to competent advice；7)负责：作为或者不作为 |

|一线： supervisors/first-line or front-line managers. 主管 team leaders 班组长 | 检查、纠正、报告：1）开工前：toolbox talks，monitor,report ;2)开工：communicate with workers,participate in risk assessments, 3)事故：low-level investigations. | 

### 2）考试重点（Claude 补充）

> ⭐ 场景题问"谁应该做什么"时，把任务和层级对上：方针、预算、文化 → 最高管理层；风险评估、程序、培训 → 经理；检查、纠正、报告 → 主管和班组长。
> ⭐ 职责可以下放，问责不能下放；最终责任在雇主和最高管理层。
> ⭐ 安全顾问（H&S team）只提供建议，不对安全负责，负责的是直线管理层。
> 内部能力不够时，要请有能力的外部专家（职业卫生师、安全工程师、职业健康顾问等）。

> 📖 教材补充（p54–55）：高层要对自己的作为和不作为负责；每年审查一次安全绩效；以身作则（参加巡查和检查、戴 PPE、关心员工）；在一些国家，高层失职会被追究法律责任。
> 📖 教材补充（p55–56）：经理的职责清单：提供并维护安全的设备；做并更新风险评估；制定规则和程序；提供培训和指示；确保足够的监督；提供 PPE；合理安排工时和休息；促进经理、工人和承包商之间的合作；参与和工人的协商；参与事故调查并整改；用监控和审核检查规定是否执行；记录和报告。
> 📖 教材补充（p56–57）：主管了解组员的能力和局限，要把工作安全地分配给有能力的人；负责事故的初步调查；主管的言行很大程度上决定组员是否重视安全。
> 📖 教材补充（p54）：共用场所或雇用承包商时，雇主的责任也覆盖这些人；反过来，其他单位和承包商也要保护你的员工。

> English:
> - Senior managers can delegate tasks, but they remain accountable for health and safety.
> - The health and safety adviser provides advice, but line managers are responsible for health and safety in their areas.
> - Supervisors should check that workers follow safe procedures and correct unsafe behaviour.

## 1.7 How Top Management Can Demonstrate Commitment（教材 p57–64）

### 1)总结

#### 1.1)commitment

> ⭐ 1）commitment：Policy 定方向	说清楚要什么 ；Provide 给资源	拿出钱、人、时间 ；Practise 做榜样	让大家看见； Performance 查结果	检查、追究、处理
> 2) resources:time,money,people,skills.
> 3) defining roles and responsibilities.
> ⭐ 4) appointing competent H&S team: 4.1)负责提供建议，不负责"管安全"。真正对安全负责的是管理层,H&S team 只是顾问;4.2) competent:knowledge + understanding +skills +experience + awareness of limits + qualifications + professional memberships.
> 5)reviews: 最高管理层定期审查安全健康绩效（periodic review of H&S performance）。
> ⭐ 6)H&S champion：董事会指定一位董事担任 H&S champion 牵头推动，但不免除其他董事和管理者的责任，法律上的最终责任始终在雇主（组织）。
> 7)collective and individual responsibilities：董事既负有集体责任（董事会作为整体），也负有个人责任（每位董事对自己的决定负责）。

### 2）考试重点（Claude 补充）

> ⭐⭐ 高频题："How can top management demonstrate commitment?" → **答题按教材的 5 个方法写（p57）**：①提供足够的资源；②明确角色和职责；③指定一位高层牵头（champion）并以身作则；④任命有能力的安全团队；⑤积极参与绩效审查。
> 4P 是帮助记忆的归纳，每个 P 可以举这些具体做法：
> Policy：签署方针、设定目标、把安全列入董事会议程；Provide：批预算、配人、给时间做培训；Practise：安全巡查、以身作则遵守规则；Performance：定期审查绩效、追究经理责任、回应警告和报告。
> 反面例子：无视安全团队的警告（叉车案例）= 缺乏承诺。
> 选外部顾问时看什么：资质、经验、专业会员资格、职业责任险、费用。

> 📖 教材补充（p62–63）：胜任能力（competence）6 要素：知识和理解、技能、经验、清楚自己能力的边界、资质、专业会员资格。
> 📖 教材补充（p63）：等专家评估期间，应**封闭受影响的区域**；选外部顾问和选内部员工用同一套能力标准，还要考虑职业责任险和费用。
> 📖 教材补充（p64）：最高管理层每年左右要审查一次绩效，看资源够不够、要不要追加投入。

> English:
> - Top management can demonstrate commitment by carrying out regular safety tours and following site rules themselves.
> - They should provide adequate resources, such as funding for training and equipment.
> - Before appointing a consultant, the employer should check their qualifications, experience and professional indemnity insurance.

### case study

> An independent health and safety consultant provided substandard advice to a client regarding risks associated with hand-arm vibration and the use of paints containing isocyanates. The advice given did not meet the expected level of a ‘competent person.’  As a result of this poor advice, the client failed to implement adequate control measures. This inaction left workers exposed to harmful levels of noise, hand-arm vibration, and chemical substances, potentially damaging their health.Upon investigation, it was discovered that the consultant lacked the proper qualifications to offer such advice. This lack of qualification led to a breach of legal obligations to the client, highlighting the importance of ensuring that health and safety consultants are fully qualified and competent in their areas of expertise.

- 问题：顾问不具备胜任能力（lacked the proper qualifications），没有达到 competent person 的标准。
- 雇主应当：聘请前核实顾问的资质、经验和专业会员资格，并确认对方投保了职业责任险（professional indemnity insurance）。
- 责任：顾问只提供建议，雇主仍然要对采取控制措施负责（the client failed to implement adequate control measures）。

### 1)总结

### 2）考试重点（Claude 补充）

> ⭐⭐ 高频："共用场所的单位应该怎么合作？" 答题框架用三个 C：communicate（互相告知危害）、coordinate（统一应急安排，明确公共区域谁负责）、collaborate（联络小组、联合检查、联合入职培训、共享资源）。
> ⭐ 合作不等于免责：每个雇主仍然要对自己的员工负责（R164 "without prejudice to"）。
> 场景里常见的问题：公共区域没人管（oversight）、各家一套疏散计划、互相不知道对方的危害（比如案例里印刷厂的易燃溶剂和隔壁的动火作业）。
> 对策清单：联络小组、书面协议明确职责（formalise）、统一应急预案和消防安全员、联合检查、联合入职培训、共享资源。

> 📖 教材补充（p67）：共用场所时每个雇主必须：告知可能受影响的人、协调预防和保护措施、共享风险信息、商定安排以避免冲突和重复。
> 📖 教材补充（p68–69）：改善合作的做法：明确职责（尤其公共区域）、写进租约（formalise）、共享安全信息、定期联席会议（liaison group）、统一应急程序、联合入职培训、共享检查结果、鼓励合作文化、**指定协调联系人**、有新租户或布局变化时**重新审查**。
> 📖 教材补充（p69）：**共同风险评估**：各家仍做自己的风险评估，但要分享相关部分；公共区域可以做联合风险评估。
> 📖 教材补充（p70）：合作的 5 个好处：标准一致、公共区域事故和未遂事件更少、应急反应更快更不混乱、关系更好、更容易证明合规。

> English:
> - The organisations should set up a liaison group to share information about hazards and agree a single emergency plan.
> - The fabrication workshop should inform the printing company before hot work starts, so that flammable materials can be moved away.

### case study

> In the scenario where two small businesses operate in the same factory building – one being a printing company using solvent-based inks and glues, and the other a fabrication workshop performing hot work – there’s a clear risk that the hot work could ignite materials used by the printing company. In this situation, both businesses have important roles to play in hazard awareness and communication.The printing company should actively assess and be aware of the hazards present around its area, particularly the flammable nature of its materials. On the other hand, the fabrication workshop should inform the printing company about the potential hazards associated with its hot work activities, such as sparks or flames that could serve as an ignition source. By sharing this information, both businesses increase their awareness of the potential fire hazards in their shared space. This mutual understanding is crucial for taking appropriate safety measures to prevent accidents. It demonstrates the importance of communication and cooperation between different businesses sharing a workspace, especially when their activities could pose risks to one another.

## 1.9 How Clients and Contractors Should Work Together（教材 p71–76）

### 1)总结

> ⭐ 1)client-contractor 双方之间都有责任：client要挑选合格承包商，监督、确保安全；contractor对自己的工作和设备负责，client出借设备的，client对出借的设备负责。对于承包商自身原因造成的损害，由承包商自己负责（操作不当导致的触电身亡）。
> 2)planning and coordination

### 2）考试重点（Claude 补充）

> ⭐⭐ 高频：发包方和承包商各自的责任。发包方（client）：选择有能力的承包商、提供现场危害信息、做入职培训、协调和监控承包商的工作、对借给承包商的设备负责。承包商（contractor）：对自己的工作、设备和员工负责，遵守现场规则。
> ⭐ 外包不等于把安全责任外包出去，双方都负有责任。
> 承包商工作风险更高的原因：不熟悉现场；工作本身风险高（拆除、维修、调试）；做事习惯不同（norms）；客户压价、催工期，导致走捷径（cutting corners）。

> 📖 教材补充（p72）：承包商的 5 个挑战：不熟悉客户的流程；文化和做事习惯不同（norms）；工作本身风险更高（拆除、维修、调试）；**人员流动大**（尤其有分包时）；容易走捷径。
> 📖 教材补充（p73）：承包商自带的设备自己负责；用客户提供的设备，由客户负责，所以一般不建议把工具借给承包商。
> 📖 教材补充（p74，ILO 建筑业实务守则 2.7）：发包方应当：指定有能力的人协调安全工作；把已知的特殊风险告知所有承包商；要求投标方在报价里留出安全费用；定工期时考虑安全要求。
> 📖 教材补充（p74）：信息交换：发包方提供现场已知的危害和风险，承包商提供**施工方案（method statement）**。

> English:
> - The client should select a competent contractor, provide information about site hazards and monitor the contractor's work.
> - The contractor is responsible for the safety of its own work and equipment, and must follow the client's site rules.
